About Me

I am currently a PhD student in the Artificial Intelligence department at Oregon State University advised by Dr. Alan Fern as part of the Dynamics Robotics and Artificial Intelligence Lab. My research is on reinforcement learning for control of bipedal and humanoid robots, working with the Cassie and Digit. My main focus is on sim-to-real for RL policies, showing new task capabilities and working on more consistent sim2real transfer.

During my undergrad at University of Washington I worked in Emo Todorov's Movement Control Laboratory working on realtime state estimation for a small Darwin OP humanoid robot and reinforcement learning for manipulation.

Outside of academic work I have also interned as a software engineer at Agility Robotics working on using reinforcement learning for control of the humanoid robot Digit. I have also interned at the RAI Institute investigating sim-to-real methods for quadruped and humanoid robots.
